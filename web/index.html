    The agent runs four steps:

    1. Read the view. An LLM turns your sentence into structured fields: ticker, direction, strength, horizon, budget and risk appetite.
    2. Get market data. Price and at-the-money implied vol, with a simple put skew added on top. The class version uses sample data; run locally for live Yahoo Finance prices.
    3. Design candidates. Three structures that fit the direction, with strikes set from the expected move (implied vol × √time). Risk reversals and collars are solved for close to zero cost.
    4. Price, score, explain. Black-Scholes prices and greeks for each leg, payoff and breakevens at expiry, market-implied chance of profit, then a fit score that rewards payoff if you're right and chance of profit, and penalises cost (if you want it cheap) and unlimited risk (if you want losses capped).

    Assumptions: European options, Black-Scholes with a linear skew, flat rates, no fees or bid/ask spread, 1 contract = 100 shares. Built for learning and demos, not investment advice.

    Prices use Black-Scholes with European exercise and no fees. For education and demos, not investment advice.
